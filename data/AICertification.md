# Certificate in the Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence

The Certificate in the Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence provides students with a foundational understanding of the mathematical and algorithmic properties of methods used in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and neural networks.

## Admission Criteria
* The certificate is open to all degree-seeking students.
* Students who are not part of an engineering professional program should contact a CSE advisor for permission.

## Program Completion Requirements
* Students must complete **12 credit hours** of required coursework listed below.
* A combined **GPA of 3.0 or better** must be earned across all courses used to satisfy the certificate requirements.
* The certificate is awarded concurrently with an undergraduate degree.

## Required Curriculum
* **CSE 4308** - ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (3 Credit Hours)
* **CSE 4309** - FUNDAMENTALS OF MACHINE LEARNING (3 Credit Hours)
* **CSE 4310** - FUNDAMENTALS OF COMPUTER VISION (3 Credit Hours)
* **CSE 4311** - NEURAL NETWORKS AND DEEP LEARNING (3 Credit Hours)
